Privacy — ChordParcel

Last updated: 11 November 2026

ChordParcel works with one chord sheet on your device. It stores your draft text, title, transposition offset and note-spelling preference locally. Your appearance preference is also stored on your device. It does not require an app account or upload sheet content, and has no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Import reads the file you choose. Export creates a new TXT or PDF copy. If you choose a cloud file provider or share a copy with another app or person, that provider or recipient handles it under their own terms. Your device backup settings may also affect local app data; ChordParcel does not provide its own cloud sync.

You can edit or replace the active draft. If a saved draft is damaged, the app may keep up to three local damaged copies for recovery. Unused temporary export copies are removed when replaced or when the export screen closes. Copies handed to a sharing destination may remain until the next app launch, when leftover temporary export copies are cleared. Deleting the app removes its local data, subject to operating-system backup behaviour. Keep an exported copy of anything important.

If you email support, we receive the address and content you send and use them to answer your request. Please do not send private sheet content unless it is needed to explain your question. For questions or deletion of support correspondence, contact tidelab.apps@gmail.com.

If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple processes testing data under its TestFlight terms. Feedback and diagnostics shared through TestFlight may be made available to Tidelab.
