ChordParcel support

Paste text with bracketed chords such as [C]Our [Am]own words, or import your UTF-8 .txt, .cho or .crd file. Choose a semitone shift, check the preview, and export a new TXT or A4 PDF copy. Reset restores the original spelling.

TXT contains only the sheet text; the title stays in the filename, local draft and PDF. Importing TXT does not restore shift settings. Reset the shift to 0 to view imported chords without transposing them again. Near the text limit, a shift can make TXT too large to save or share. The app explains this before transfer: use PDF, shorten the text or change the shift.

Supported chord suffixes: none, m, 7, m7, maj7, dim, aug, sus2, sus4, add9. A slash bass note is supported. Chords must use A–G with an optional # or b; ♯ and ♭ are accepted. Full ChordPro directives and chords above syllables are not supported. Correct flagged input before export. Limits: 64 KB UTF-8, 200 lines, 2,000 chord tokens.

One active draft is kept. Replacing it requires confirmation; export anything you want to keep. If a cloud file provider is unavailable, try a local file. For help, email tidelab.apps@gmail.com with your app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce. Share a short original example instead of private or copyrighted material.
