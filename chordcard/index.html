ChordParcel

Transpose your own chord sheet. Share the new copy.

In development. Not yet available on the App Store.

Paste your own text with chords in square brackets, choose a semitone shift, and preview a new TXT or PDF copy. ChordParcel works with one sheet, without a song catalogue. Use material you own or have permission to use.
