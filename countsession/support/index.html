CountSession support

CountSession is currently in development and is not available on the App Store.

Add items to your list and enter their expected quantities. Start a check and enter an actual quantity for every item. Enter 0 explicitly if none remain. Review the differences, correct any count, then confirm completion to save the result in history.

Only one check can be open at a time. Return to it to continue, or confirm that you want to discard it. Finish or discard the open check before changing your list. Completed checks cannot be edited or deleted within the app; updating your list does not change them.

CountSession has one list of up to 100 items. Quantities must be whole numbers from 0 to 999999. It has no sync, import or export. Standard iPhone backups may include its data, depending on your settings; CountSession has no separate backup or restore tool. Deleting the app removes its local data.

If a change cannot be saved, the app keeps the last successfully saved state. Check the error, make sure your device has available storage, and retry. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step if you need to keep its local data. If you see a notice about an unreadable saved file, the app has kept that file separately and started empty; automatic recovery is not promised.

Need help? Email tidelab.apps@gmail.com with your iOS version, app version, the error message and steps to reproduce the problem. Please avoid sending personal item lists or quantities. App purchases and refund requests are handled through Apple: https://support.apple.com/118223.
