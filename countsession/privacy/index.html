CountSession privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

CountSession is provided by Tidelab. Contact: tidelab.apps@gmail.com.

CountSession stores your item names, expected quantities, counts and completed checks locally on your device. The app does not send this content to Tidelab or an analytics service. It has no app account, advertising, tracking, telemetry or third-party analytics SDK. It has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Saved counts remain on your device between sessions. Completed checks cannot be edited or deleted within the app. If a saved file is damaged or uses a newer data format that this version cannot read, the app keeps that file separately on your device and starts with an empty list, with a notice. This does not recover the unreadable file's contents.

CountSession works offline. It does not include cloud sync or a separate backup, import or export feature. Its local files are eligible for the standard iPhone backup, depending on your device's backup settings. Apple manages system backups; Tidelab does not receive them. Deleting the app removes its local data, including retained unreadable files. Copies in device backups are managed separately through your system backup settings. Do not rely on CountSession to preserve data after app deletion or to restore it from a backup.

The purchase and download of the app are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. CountSession does not receive your payment details.

If you email tidelab.apps@gmail.com, your email provider sends us the message and address you provide. We use them to respond to your support request. This correspondence is separate from the app's local data. Please do not send sensitive item lists or quantities. For privacy questions or to request deletion of a support message, contact tidelab.apps@gmail.com.
