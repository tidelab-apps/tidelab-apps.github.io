SRTPatch support

SRTPatch is a Tidelab app currently in development and is not available on the App Store.

Open one UTF-8 .srt file from Files, up to 2,000,000 bytes and 5,000 subtitle entries. Review the reported timing, empty-subtitle and numbering issues. Invalid input must be corrected before export. Overlaps can be intentional and need your confirmation; any numbering changes need your explicit choice.

Enter a positive or negative shift in whole milliseconds. Apply it to all subtitles or a range of positions in the file. A shift that would produce an invalid time is rejected as a whole. This corrects a constant offset; it does not correct timing drift.

Compare the original and resulting start and end times before saving or sharing a new copy. Your source file is left unchanged. Export uses UTF-8, LF line endings, sequential numbering and no BOM, while preserving subtitle text, line breaks, Unicode and literal tags. Choose the destination in the system save or share interface. Cloud providers and sharing services may need a connection and handle the file under their own rules.

SRTPatch works with SRT only. It does not play video, transcribe or translate speech, burn subtitles into video or convert other subtitle formats. For a rejected file, check its encoding, size and reported errors. For a failed save or share, keep your original, check the destination and its available storage or connection, then try again. Do not treat the temporary working copy as a backup.

Need help? Email tidelab.apps@gmail.com with your app version, iOS version, the exact error and steps to reproduce it. Please do not send confidential subtitle files. If needed, include a small example you have permission to share, with personal information removed. See the Privacy Policy for how support correspondence is handled.
