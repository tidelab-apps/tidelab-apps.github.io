SRTPatch privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

SRTPatch is a Tidelab app currently in development. It is not available on the App Store. This policy describes its current implementation. Contact: tidelab.apps@gmail.com.

SRTPatch checks and shifts one user-selected UTF-8 SRT subtitle file on your device. The app does not send subtitle contents to Tidelab or run an account, analytics, advertising or tracking service. It has no third-party SDK, AI service or automatic content sync.

Between sessions, the app keeps four local settings: the last time shift, whether a range is selected, and the range's first and last positions. It does not keep subtitle contents, filenames, file paths or a file history in these settings. Choosing new values replaces the previous settings.

Import creates a temporary working copy. Saving or sharing prepares a new copy without changing your original. Temporary working copies are removed when their session ends; copies needed by an active system save or share operation remain until that operation finishes or is dismissed. Leftovers from interrupted sessions are removed on the next launch. Moving the app into the background does not itself delete these files. Cleanup is ordinary file removal, not a guarantee of secure erasure.

You choose the file source and the save or share destination. A cloud Files provider, iCloud or a sharing recipient may transfer or store a file under its own rules. Exported copies remain in the destination you choose; the app's temporary cleanup does not delete them. You manage those copies through the destination service. Apple manages system diagnostics and device backups separately under its own privacy terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

Contacting support is optional and separate from processing a file in the app. The app does not automatically send email. If you write to tidelab.apps@gmail.com, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send, and use them to respond to your request. Our mailbox uses Gmail; Google and your email provider process the correspondence under their own policies. Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. Please do not send confidential subtitle files or other sensitive information. For privacy questions or a request to delete support correspondence, email the same address. We do not promise a fixed retention period or deletion of copies controlled by email providers.

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. The pages contain no analytics, scripts, cookies or contact forms added by Tidelab, and load their styles and images from this site. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, including visits without a GitHub account. GitHub handles hosting data under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Website visits and support emails are separate from the app's local subtitle processing.
