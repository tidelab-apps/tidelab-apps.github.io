JPEGCeiling privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-11-10

JPEGCeiling processes the photo you select on your device. Tidelab does not receive your photos through JPEGCeiling. The app does not require an account. Between sessions, it keeps only your last valid numeric size settings, not a photo history.

While a result is open, the app retains a temporary input file and keeps the prepared JPEG in memory so you can share or save again. Closing the result, selecting another photo, cancelling import or preparation, an import or preparation error, or entering the background outside a system operation clears the working photo and result. Changing limits from the result discards that result but keeps the selected photo for another run.

Files needed for an active system picker, share or export operation remain available until it finishes or is cancelled. The separate temporary file used for sharing or exporting is removed when that operation ends. Sharing or saving does not by itself close the result or clear the input copy. Leftover temporary files are removed on the next launch after a crash. Copies you explicitly export remain in the destination you choose.

Source metadata is not copied to the output. Importing an item from iCloud or another provider, or sharing through another service, is handled by that provider under its own policy. Contact: tidelab.apps@gmail.com.
