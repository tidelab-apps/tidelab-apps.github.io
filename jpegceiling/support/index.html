JPEGCeiling support

Choose one JPEG or supported HEIC photo from Photos or Files. Enter a positive whole-number file size limit in KB and a maximum long edge in pixels. 1 KB is 1,000 bytes. The app may reduce dimensions further to meet the file size limit. Preview the result and check its bytes and dimensions before sharing or saving the JPEG.

PNG, animation and batch processing are not supported. If you cancel import or preparation, or an import or preparation error occurs, choose the photo again before retrying. If preparation fails, try a larger file size limit or a different supported photo. A website may have other requirements; acceptance is not guaranteed. A cloud-only photo may need to download before import.

For help, email tidelab.apps@gmail.com with the app version, iOS version, your numeric limits and the error message. Please do not send personal photos or sensitive documents.
