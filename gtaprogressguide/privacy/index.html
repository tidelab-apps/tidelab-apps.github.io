The app has no app account, advertisements, analytics or tracking SDKs, AI features or model downloads. It does not request access to your location, photos or contacts. Automatic catalogue requests, Apple services, external websites and email support are separate flows described below.

Data on your device

Your personal checklist items, completion marks, language, platform, onboarding and spoiler preferences are stored locally as versioned JSON in the app's Application Support folder. The app starts with bundled content and can use a validated locally saved active or last-good catalogue. There is no app-operated cloud synchronisation of your checklist.

Saved generations, including damaged or incomplete generations, can remain on the device to support recovery. Resetting items, marks or preferences changes the current state; it is not a secure erase of every older local generation. Restore preview files are scheduled for removal when you confirm or cancel the restore; file-removal errors or an interrupted operation may leave local files.

Automatic catalogue checks

On launch and return to the foreground, the app automatically checks whether a catalogue update is due, at most once per six hours. It sends an HTTPS GET to https://tidelab-apps.github.io/gta6/content/v1/manifest.json. Failed checks also record a local check time. Checks are skipped during read-only recovery.

An eligible newer manifest can trigger a GET for a data package from the same GitHub Pages origin under /gta6/content/v1/packages/. The app checks its path, size, checksum and content before activation. Redirects are rejected. Unavailable or invalid updates leave bundled or last-good content in use. These are data updates, not executable code or purchase rights.

Catalogue update requests do not upload progress, personal checklist text, preferences or purchase entitlements. They add no installation identifier or personal-data request body or query. They can send a saved catalogue ETag to check for changes.

The host receives your IP address and ordinary connection/request metadata, which may include a default User-Agent and TLS information. GitHub Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security even without a GitHub login. Hosting logs are subject to the hosting provider's retention practices. GitHub privacy information: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

GitHub receives the connection data described above when the app checks its catalogue and when you visit this website. GitHub's published privacy statement describes processing in the United States and other countries, and reliance on Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable. Its “International data transfers” and “Data Privacy Framework (DPF)” sections explain those safeguards and how to obtain further information. Its “Security and Retention” section explains the criteria for its own retention. See https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement .

The last check time, outcome and accepted ETag are stored locally. The bundled catalogue contains 20 informational facts and three guides. An update may be unavailable; no update schedule is promised.

Backup files and your choices

A JSON backup is exported only when you ask. You choose a destination or recipient through Apple's system share sheet or Files provider. The file contains checklist data and preferences, so share it only with people and services you trust. A provider you select may upload or synchronise its copy under its own terms; this is separate from the app and Tidelab does not receive that copy.

Sharing creates a temporary export file in a uniquely named folder. The app does not immediately delete this file after sharing; its cleanup timing is managed by the operating system. Copies saved or sent to other destinations remain there until you or that service remove them.

Import lets you preview a backup before confirming replacement of the current tracker data; it does not merge lists. You can cancel before replacement. Settings offers separate confirmed resets for published marks, personal items and preferences. Deleting the app may remove its local data. Device backups, exported files and provider copies must be managed separately; no complete or immediate erasure of those copies is promised. Tidelab cannot retrieve local checklist data that you have not sent to us.

Apple purchases

Apple handles App Store transactions through StoreKit under its own privacy terms: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. The app reads verified transaction and entitlement information locally to determine access. Purchase rights are not stored as a user-editable Pro flag and are not included in a checklist backup. Tidelab does not receive your payment card details from the app. Purchase-related requests to Apple are separate from checklist storage; this policy does not claim that Apple processes no data. Purchases can be initiated when the catalogue is ready and Apple makes the relevant StoreKit products available.

External websites and this site

A source website is opened when you choose its link; automatic catalogue checks are the separate flow described above. The destination may receive technical data such as your IP address and has its own privacy policy. This Tidelab website adds no scripts, cookies, analytics or externally loaded media. GitHub Pages hosts it and logs visitors' IP addresses for security; hosting data is governed by GitHub's privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Tidelab does not control every log kept by external operators.

The GitHub processing locations, transfer information and retention criteria described under Automatic catalogue checks also apply to visits to this website.
