About this app

Unofficial app for fans. A personal checklist and independent information guide.

Unofficial fan app. Not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

Guide and catalogue

The bundled catalogue has 20 informational facts and three guides. It is not a complete walkthrough and does not supply verified gameplay, mission, collectible or cheat-code coverage, a game map or access to the game.

The app automatically checks for catalogue updates on launch or return to the foreground, at most once per six hours. An eligible same-origin package is used only after validation. A failed check or validation leaves bundled or last-good content in use and does not upload your checklist. Updates may be unavailable.

Purchases require a ready catalogue and products available from Apple through StoreKit. If the catalogue is not ready or products cannot be loaded, a new purchase may be unavailable. Check the message in the app and try again when the catalogue and Apple connection are available.

Checklist and preferences

Create your own items and mark them complete. Your items, editorial completion marks and preferences stay on this device. Settings lets you change language, platform and spoiler preferences. Spoilers require confirmation before being enabled. There is no app account or automatic cloud sync.

Export and restore

In Settings, use Export backup, then Share backup to choose a destination. Keep a copy somewhere you control before resetting data or deleting the app. The JSON file can contain your personal item text; avoid posting it publicly.

To restore, select a backup file and review its preview. Confirm only if you want to replace the current data, rather than merge it. Cancel to leave the current data unchanged. Spoilers are hidden after restore. A checklist backup does not restore Pro purchase rights; those are checked separately through Apple.

Reset published marks, Reset my items and Reset preferences each require confirmation and affect their named category. Earlier local generations may remain for recovery. Exported files and copies in other services are managed separately.

If saving or importing fails

Read the displayed error and preserve your current installation and any existing backup. Do not delete the app as a troubleshooting step if you need its local data. An invalid backup is not applied. If a last-good recovery state is offered, review and confirm it before continuing; it may be older than your latest changes. Recovery is not guaranteed for every damaged file.

Apple purchase controls

Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account used for the purchase. A checklist import is not a purchase restore. Restore or product loading may need a connection to Apple; an unavailable product does not mean a new purchase is required.

Manage subscriptions opens Apple's subscription controls. Apple explains cancellation at https://support.apple.com/118428 and refund requests at https://support.apple.com/118223. Apple decides eligibility; Tidelab does not promise a refund. Existing marks, personal items and backup are preserved when Pro ends.
