Decklet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-01

In short

Decklet does not collect your data. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. Your decks, cards, notes, photos, scans and study history stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your decks, cards, review history, settings and the data for the widgets are stored on your iPhone only (in the app and in the app group that the app and its widget share).

Decklet has no server of its own and does not send this data anywhere. The app makes no network connections of its own.

Camera, photos and PDF files

"Scan to cards" uses Apple's document camera. The text on the pages is read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. The page pictures are shown as previews while the screen is open; they are not saved and not sent anywhere.

When you choose photos, the app receives only the pictures you pick. PDF files are read on the device.

Cards written by AI are made by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence). Your notes, photos and scans are not sent to us or to any other company by Decklet.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple (App Store). Decklet never sees your payment details; it only learns from Apple whether Pro is active. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Reminders

The daily reminder is a notification created on your iPhone. It is off until you turn it on.

Backups and sharing

A backup file or a CSV export leaves the app only when you choose where to save or send it in the iOS share sheet. Decklet does not upload it.

What we do not do

No account or sign-in. No advertising. No tracking across apps or websites. No analytics or crash-reporting services. No third-party code in the app. We do not sell or share data, because we do not have any.

Children

Decklet collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its data is removed from your iPhone. A backup file that you saved elsewhere stays where you saved it until you delete it.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example if a feature needs a network connection), we will update this page and the privacy answers in the App Store before the new version is released.
