Decklet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-06

In short

Decklet does not collect your data. There is no account, no tracking, no advertising and no analytics. Your decks, cards, notes, photos, scans and study history stay on your iPhone.

What stays on your device

Your decks, cards, review history, settings and the data for the widgets are stored on your iPhone only (in the app and in the app group that the app and its widget share).

Decklet has no server of its own and does not send this content anywhere. The optional language-model download and purchases use an internet connection, as described below.

Camera, photos and PDF files

"Scan to cards" uses Apple's document camera. The text on the pages is read on your iPhone with Apple's Vision framework. The page pictures are shown as previews while the screen is open; they are not saved and not sent anywhere.

When you choose photos, the app receives only the pictures you pick. PDF files are read on the device.

Cards written by AI are made on your iPhone, using Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence) when available or a downloaded language model. Your notes, photos, scans and PDFs are not sent to us or to any other company by Decklet.

Language-model download

On iPhones with enough memory, if Apple's model is unavailable or does not support the language of your text, Decklet can download the open Qwen3.5 2B language model (about 1.3 GB) from Hugging Face (https://huggingface.co/). This one-time download starts only when you tap Download and uses Wi-Fi. The file's size and SHA-256 checksum are checked before it is used. After the download, AI works offline on your iPhone.

The download sends no cards, notes, photos, scans, PDFs or other user content to Hugging Face. As with any download, the hosting provider receives your IP address and the time of the request. We do not receive this information.

Open-source components

Decklet includes llama.cpp (MIT licence) to run the downloaded Qwen3.5 model (Apache 2.0 licence) on your iPhone. These components process your content on the device, not on a remote AI service.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time purchase are handled by Apple (App Store). Decklet never sees your payment details; it only learns from Apple whether Pro is active. Apple's own privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Reminders

The daily reminder is a notification created on your iPhone. It is off until you turn it on.

Backups and sharing

A backup file or a CSV export leaves the app only when you choose where to save or send it in the iOS share sheet. Decklet does not upload it.

What we do not do

No account or sign-in. No advertising. No tracking across apps or websites. No analytics or crash-reporting services. We do not sell or share your content, because we do not receive it.

Children

Decklet collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its data is removed from your iPhone. A backup file that you saved elsewhere stays where you saved it until you delete it.

Changes

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this page and the privacy answers in the App Store before the new version is released.
