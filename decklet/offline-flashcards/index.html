Move a small flashcard deck without creating an account

From the small team at Tidelab that makes Decklet · 9 November 2026

Want to keep studying your own cards without another sign-in? Start with a small export you can inspect. Decklet lets you import a CSV and study on your iPhone without an account.

Try five everyday Spanish words

Here is a small original example. The front asks for the Spanish word; the back gives the answer. Import it as a deck named Everyday Spanish.

front,back
the key,la llave
the door,la puerta
the window,la ventana
the table,la mesa
the chair,la silla

Move a test deck first

  1. If you already have cards, export a copy from your current tool. Keep the original file and collection.
  2. For this workflow, use a UTF-8 CSV with the question in column one and the answer in column two. Copy five representative text cards into a small test file.
  3. In Decklet choose Import, then CSV or TSV. Choose the file, or paste the two-column text. Turn on First row is a header if your first row contains column names.
  4. Check that the preview shows the expected fronts and backs, five cards found and no skipped rows. Name the deck and tap Import.
  5. Open a study session. For the key, try to recall la llave before revealing the answer. Rate what you actually remembered.

Check what transfers

This two-column CSV carries front and back text. Do not expect it to carry images, audio, tags or your previous study schedule. Inspect your test deck before moving a larger collection.

Decklet also offers .apkg import, but this guide uses CSV so you can inspect the example in any spreadsheet. A successful text import is not proof that a different file format will preserve every part of a collection.

Keep a copy you control

Export a deck as CSV when you want a portable text copy; use Decklet's file backup for a separate backup. Keep those files somewhere you choose. No account also means there is no Decklet cloud account to restore your cards from.

What you need

Decklet requires an iPhone running iOS 26 or later. Manual cards, CSV import and spaced repetition are free without daily limits or ads. No account is needed, and studying works offline. AI creation is optional Pro functionality and is not needed for this example.
