Turn a spreadsheet into flashcards on your iPhone

From the small team that makes Decklet · 7 November 2026

You do not need AI to turn a vocabulary list into study cards. A two-column spreadsheet is enough: put a question or word in the first column and its answer in the second.

Make a small file first

Start with five rows. Keep each card focused on one thing you want to recall. Save the sheet as a UTF-8 CSV file, or use tab-separated text. Decklet reads the first two columns as front and back.

front,back
hello,bonjour
thank you,merci
please,s'il vous plaît
water,eau
the station,la gare

Download this five-card example. We wrote these simple travel vocabulary pairs for you to try; no sign-up is needed.

Import and check

  1. Open Decklet and choose Import, then CSV or TSV.
  2. Choose the file, or paste the two-column text.
  3. If the first row contains column names, turn on “First row is a header”.
  4. Check the preview and the number of skipped rows. Give the deck a name, then tap Import.
  5. Start studying and check the answer before you rate your recall.

If a row looks wrong

Check that both columns have text. A comma inside a question or answer needs CSV quotes; exporting from your spreadsheet handles that for you. Try a five-row file before importing a larger collection, and keep the original file.

Share the material you choose

You can export a deck as CSV and share that file using the iPhone share sheet. The person receiving it can inspect it in a spreadsheet or import it into Decklet. Share only material you created or have permission to distribute; leave out personal information.

What is free

Manual cards, CSV import and spaced repetition are free in Decklet, without a daily card limit. AI card creation is an optional Pro feature and is not needed for this guide.
