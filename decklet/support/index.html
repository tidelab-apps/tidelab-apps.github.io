Decklet Support

Last updated: 2026-11-01

Contact

Write to tidelab.apps@gmail.com. Please tell us your iPhone model, the iOS version and what happened.

Restore purchases

Settings > Restore purchases (also on the purchase screen). Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

Manage or cancel a subscription

iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Decklet Pro. Cancelling stops the renewal; Pro stays until the end of the paid period.

Cards with AI do not work

The AI needs Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later) turned on in iPhone Settings. Without it, Decklet offers "Split into cards", which turns lines like "term - meaning" into cards by hand. Studying itself is always free.

Move my cards

Settings > Backup and restore makes a file that you can restore on another iPhone. A deck can also be exported as CSV. Decks from other flashcard apps (.apkg) can be imported from the "+" button.

Delete my data

Delete the app. Decklet keeps nothing outside your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
