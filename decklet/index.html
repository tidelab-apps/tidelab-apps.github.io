Decklet

Private, offline study cards

Available on the App Store

Decklet is a flashcard app with spaced repetition for iPhone. Your notes stay on your iPhone.

  • No account
  • No tracking
  • No analytics
  • Works offline
✓

Study free, without limits

Spaced repetition (FSRS) shows each card just before you would forget it. Any number of decks and cards, no daily limit, no ads.

✓

Start in one tap

Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets and a Control Center button start a session. Daily reminder, streak and statistics.

✦

Cards from your own material

Scan a page, choose photos or PDF files, or paste your notes. AI writes the cards on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence, and you check them before they are saved.
